# Product specifications

Record eligibility, cooldown, pricing, ownership, and moderation rules here before implementing their respective features. No such rules are implied by the bootstrap.

[Competitive landscape and substitute map (EGO-68)](competitive-landscape.md)
records 20 mechanism analogs across 17 platforms, dated primary sources,
MVP recommendations and differentiators. The portable
[comparison matrix](competitive-landscape.json) distinguishes documented mechanics
from untested interpretations. Its [product risks](product-risks.md) are initial
inputs for EGO-71; [product hypotheses](product-hypotheses.md) propose validation
methods for EGO-69/70/67 and the gated pilot. Proposed criteria are not accepted
launch gates, measured demand or authorization to enable real payments.

[Acquiring provider readiness (EGO-99)](payment-provider-decision.md) records the
repository audit, ЮKassa/Т-Бизнес decision inputs, merchant website gaps and
human-only onboarding checklist. The [offline acquiring cost model](payment-economics.md)
compares configurable fee assumptions. ЮKassa is the selected primary, Т-Бизнес
the fallback; ЮKassa sandbox credentials are stored externally in Lockbox.
Payment runtime and production activation remain deferred.
